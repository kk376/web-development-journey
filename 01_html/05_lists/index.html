Lists

Lists are used to group a collection of items. There are two types of lists in HTML: ordered lists (ol) and unordered lists (ul).

Ordered Lists: The items are numbered with a sequential number like 1, 2, 3...

  1. First item
  2. Second item
  3. Third item

Unordered Lists: The items are bulleted with a dot or other symbols.

  • First item
  • Second item
  • Third item
